Anniversary & birthday statues

Milestones Deserve More Than Flowers.

25th anniversary. 50th. The big 6-0. Mark it with a hand-sculpted statue of the people being celebrated — the gift that outlives the occasion.

Milestone occasions call for milestone gifts. An anniversary 3D statue turns the couple — or the birthday star — into a hand-painted keepsake sculpted from their photographs, capturing them at their celebrated best.

It works for silver jubilees, golden anniversaries, retirements and milestone birthdays: personal where cash is cold, permanent where flowers fade.

  • Recreate wedding-day looks from old photographs
  • Couple, solo or full-family anniversary pieces
  • Engraved base plate with names & date on request
  • Preview approval + insured pan-India delivery

The 25th & 50th, done right

Parents' silver and golden anniversaries are the most gifted statues we make. Recreate their wedding-day look from old photos, or sculpt them as they are today — either way, it becomes the centrepiece of the celebration. Pair with a full family statue for the ultimate surprise.

Birthdays & retirements too

A beloved teacher's retirement. A father's 60th. A friend's 50th. Any milestone where the person is the occasion deserves their likeness in 3D — group gifting makes it easy to commission together.
